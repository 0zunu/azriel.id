Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is a business management system that integrates various functions and processes within an organization. ERP helps companies manage resources, improve operational efficiency, and provide accurate information for decision-making. With ERP, companies can automate business processes, enhance collaboration between departments, and increase overall productivity.
